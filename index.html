Help & How It Works
Quick answers for the core Zidea flows.
How do voice calls work?

Open a conversation or profile card, tap Call, and allow microphone access. Zidea connects the call in-browser and shows a timer once the peer connection is active.

How does the collaboration matcher work?

Generate Ideas asks a few structured questions, saves your idea, then scores real submissions by category, audience overlap, skills, collaborator need, and weekly commitment.

How do I play games?

Go to Arcade to play solo against the bot or switch to multiplayer mode. During a call, use the Play button to open a lightweight in-call game dock.

What can ZiD AI do?

ZiD AI can brainstorm, explain Zidea features, help shape an idea, draft messages, and answer general questions when an API key is configured.

How do I reset my password?

On the login modal, choose Forgot password? and enter your account email. Zidea starts a reset request and shows confirmation without exposing whether an email exists.

How do I delete my account?

Open Contact from the footer or email hello@zidea.app from your account email. Include your @username and request account deletion so support can verify and remove your data.
